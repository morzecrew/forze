# T-0074 — Drain announcement and readiness checks

Executing all three phases of [RFC 0074](../rfcs/0074-drain-announcement-and-readiness-checks.md)
on `feat/drain-announcement`: readiness waits for lifecycle startup, `runtime.begin_draining()`
withdraws readiness without refusing work, registered drain phases run between that and
teardown, the probe listener gains dependency checks through one shared evaluator, and
`forze_fastapi.serve_uvicorn` routes SIGTERM into that order.

The author is unavailable for this run. Per the orchestrator's rules, OPEN rows and unlisted
gaps are decided and logged here with a proposed row; flag-dont-flip's readiness gate (three
or more load-bearing gaps means stop) is waived by those rules, and the gaps below are
decided rather than escalated.
