# T-0071 — Lifetime leadership, phases 1 and 2

Executing phases 1 and 2 of [RFC 0071](../rfcs/0071-lifetime-leadership.md) on
`feat/lifetime-leadership`: a lifecycle step that elects one replica to run a body for as long
as it holds a renewing lease, with per-term owners, two deadlines measured from the send time,
release after the body, the late-acquire rule, the `claim` hook, `term.token`, `may_campaign`,
`verify`, the metrics, and handoff on request through `{key}:want`. Phase 3 (`fence_floor`)
waits for the fence-advance capability (RFC 0072), which is not on this base; until then the
step refuses a `fence_floor` (entry 2).

The author is away, so every gap below is decided here and carries a proposed row.
