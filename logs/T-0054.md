# T-0054 — Civil time

Executing [RFC 0054](../rfcs/0054-civil-time.md) on `feat/civil-time` — its one phase: the
value object, the six functions, the three-zone DST battery and the docs section. Started under
the maintainer's standing instruction to execute orthogonal RFCs overnight without a go; where a
question would normally be put, the RFC's own leaning or recommended option is taken and said so.

## Plan

- `forze/base/primitives/civil_time.py` (new, beside `Period` — see the D-6 entry): `CivilZone`
  (one IANA key, refused at construction when unknown), `to_instant` refusing `dst_ambiguous`
  (unless `fold` is given) and `dst_nonexistent` as `precondition` (D-1), `local_day_bounds` and
  `month_bounds` returning `Period[datetime]` with `bounds="[)"` and a nonexistent local midnight
  resolved forward (D-3), `spanned_local_days`, `elapsed_minutes` refusing naive input (D-4), and
  `AwareDatetime` for an app's boundary models (D-5).
- Battery: every function across the spring-forward and fall-back days of `Europe/Berlin`,
  `America/Santiago` (midnight transition) and `Australia/Lord_Howe` (30-minute transition), per §6.
- Docs: a section beside the `Period` docs, with the refusal table and the arithmetic trap; changelog.

Read before code: §5's rejected alternatives — picking the earlier instant, a process-wide zone,
extending the analytics bucketing — each is the shape the implementation will reach for.

## Self-audit — 2026-09-29

Scope: the branch against `main` — 3 commits, one new module in `forze.base.primitives`, its
battery, the docs section and the changelog. Re-read RFC 0054 §5, §6 and §11 first; every §6 leg
exists, across Berlin, Santiago and Lord Howe as the RFC names them.

Sabotage, then coverage: 17 mutants over the conversions, the day and month bounds, the range
walk, the duration and the boundary type. 15 killed; the two survivors were equivalent, not
untested — an early return for an empty range (the day walk already yields nothing for it —
deleted), and requiring only the first of the two fold readings to fail before refusing a skipped
time (under PEP 495 a skipped time reads back under neither fold, so no input tells the two apart;
kept, as the symmetric condition says what it means).

What coverage found is the finding worth keeping: **the gap search had a branch no zone in the
battery reached.** Every gap in the named zones starts exactly at midnight, so the transition is
always the later reading. A scan of the zone database found no gap straddling a midnight since
1970 — but six in 1919 (Toronto and its aliases jumped from 23:30 to 00:30), where neither reading
of midnight is the day's first instant. That day is now a leg, and replacing the search with the
later reading fails it and nothing else. A repeated midnight (Havana, 1 Nov 2026) is a leg too; it
had no case in the named zones either. 100% of lines and branches.

Residue I would still distrust: a zone's rules can change between `tzdata` releases (§8), and a
stored local time reinterprets while a stored instant does not; the battery pins 2026 transitions
as the installed database has them.
