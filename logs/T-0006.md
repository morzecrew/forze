# T-0006 — RFC 0006 P1: the `openai_chat` wire protocol

Executing [RFC 0006](../rfcs/0006-llm-adapters-token-streaming.md) phase P1 on
`feat/openai-chat-protocol`: one more dialect on the shipped inference-HTTP plane, prompt as
route config, no new package and no provider client.

Grades are copied from the RFC's §8 table as it stood at `7f05c10d5`. Three of the entries below
are `unlisted` rather than departures from rows 5 and 8: those rows lock *what* the constraint is
derived from and *where* a refusal maps to, and are honored as written — what they do not settle is
the strictness of the constraint sent and the response field a refusal is read from. Close-outs for
both locked areas are appended once the compliant code exists.
