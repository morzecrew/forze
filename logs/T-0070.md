# T-0070 — Outbox consume mode, phase 1

Executing phase 1 of [RFC 0070](../rfcs/0070-outbox-consume-mode.md) on
`feat/outbox-consume-mode`: the `storage="consume"` setting and its refusals, the pass scope
and the relay core entering it, the consume variants of the store methods, the background
step's tick end and backoff, `pass_deadline` and `publish_timeout`, the DST conformance leg,
the Postgres integration tests of §6 (no delivery log, no load comparison) and the schema page.
The delivery log (phase 2) and the load comparison (phase 3) are out of scope.

The author is unavailable, so every gap below is decided and logged with a proposed row.
Three of them are load-bearing (entries 1, 2 and 6), which is the readiness gate's threshold;
the build rules for this run say to decide and carry on rather than stop, so the proposals
are the questions this build would otherwise have asked.
