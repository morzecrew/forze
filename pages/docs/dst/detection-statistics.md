# Detection statistics

DST's claims about itself are measured, not asserted. The harness is exercised against a
**contract-misuse corpus** — hand-authored known-bug twins of correct Forze workloads (a dropped
rev guard, an outbox write outside its transaction, a retry without its idempotency key), each
paired with a known-correct control — so every number below has ground truth behind it: the
defect is known to be there, and for every transferable mutant its manifestation is verified
against real Postgres, not only the mock.

Detection time is survival analysis: a campaign runs seeds until the first detection or a
censoring ceiling, and the results are quantiles and exact intervals — never means over
heavy-tailed, censored data. The full protocol runs 300 independent campaigns per (mutant,
strategy); the whole dataset reproduces from one master seed.

## How fast is a bug found?

The depth-2 mutant — a torn two-transaction activation whose observation needs a genuine
scheduler overtake, not mere concurrent overlap — is where exploration strategy is theoretically
interesting:

![Kaplan–Meier detection curves for the depth-2 mutant](./_images/campaign_km_light.svg#only-light)
![Kaplan–Meier detection curves for the depth-2 mutant](./_images/campaign_km_dark.svg#only-dark)

Half the campaigns detect within 3 seeds; every campaign detects well before the 2000-seed
ceiling. The three strategies are statistically comparable here — and on depth-1 bugs that is
exactly what PCT's theory predicts, since its depth parameter buys nothing when a bug needs zero
ordering constraints.

## Per-seed detection probability, per mutant

![Per-seed detection probability with exact 95% intervals](./_images/campaign_forest_light.svg#only-light)
![Per-seed detection probability with exact 95% intervals](./_images/campaign_forest_dark.svg#only-dark)

The spread is the corpus design, not noise: collision-pool workloads deliberately make the
trigger rare (p̂ ≈ 1/16) so strategies have something to discriminate; crash-fault mutants sit at
the crash-point lottery's rate; deterministic-manifestation mutants (a dropped tenant filter
leaks on essentially every seed) sit at 1. Known-correct controls produced **zero false
positives** across every strategy at 400 runs per cell — reported with its exact upper bound,
never as a bare zero.

The canonical data views, regenerated by `just dst-campaign` and the analysis script, are the
[campaign results page](campaign-results.md) — including the p̂-versus-PCT-bound
analysis, where the measured detection rates respect the published guarantee in every applicable
cell and the depth-2 cell shows the bound doing real work (respected, and conservative by ~50×).
